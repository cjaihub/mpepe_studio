# Oneova brand kit

**Name:** Oneova (never "Mpepe" or "Oneover")
**Motto:** Is the reason you matter.

## Colors
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Brand Blue | `#0C4FA8` |
| Accent (badges, live, highlights, secondary buttons) | Brand Pink | `#FF2D87` |
| Text / dark backgrounds | Ink | `#0A0A0A` |
| Light backgrounds | White | `#FFFFFF` |

Blue stays dominant; pink is only an accent. Never use maroon or red as branding.

## Type
Inter (Google Fonts) for app and web, bold 800 for the wordmark. Helvetica Neue or Arial are acceptable fallbacks.

## What's in the kit
- `logo/`: stacked and horizontal logos, on white and on blue
- `icon/`: the round app icon with a transparent background, 48 to 512 px
- `wordmark/`: the "Oneova" name alone, blue (transparent) and white on blue
- `social/`: profile picture 800, link-share image 1200×630, cover 1500×500, Play feature graphic 1024×500
- `favicon/`: 16, 32 and Apple touch icon 180
- `google/`: the sign-in (OAuth) consent-screen logo, 120 and 512
- `colors/`: palette swatch

## Use
- Leave clear space around the logo of at least a quarter of the icon's width.
- Use the white-text versions on blue or dark backgrounds only.
- Don't stretch, recolor, rotate, or add effects to the icon or wordmark.
