## 1. Scope and Global + African Data Protection Standards

## 3. How We Protect AI, Biometrics & Facial Landmarks

**3.1 On-Device AR & Beauty Processing.** When you use Studio Looks, Boost Beauty, or AR camera lenses, facial/pose landmark alignment runs in volatile device memory in real time to position visual effects. **Oneova does not build or sell a facial-recognition identity database from camera effects.**

**3.2 App Biometric Lock.** When you enable Fingerprint or Face Unlock in **Privacy & Security**, authentication is performed strictly by your operating system's secure hardware enclave (`Android Keystore` / `Apple Secure Enclave`). Oneova never receives or stores your fingerprint image or facial scan.

**3.3 AI Firewall & Zero Private Training.** Inputs handled by Oneova AI tools pass through `AiSecurityGateway` prompt-injection isolation. Private Chat messages, calls, KYC documents, and unpublished Studio drafts are **never** used to train generative AI models or shared with external advertisers.

## 4. How and When Information Is Shared

We **never sell or rent** personal data to data brokers or third parties. We share limited data only in five strictly controlled situations:

1. **According to Your Audience Settings:** Other users see your profile, public Reels, Live broadcasts, or Biz store according to whether you choose **Public**, **Followers Only**, or **Private**.
2. **Biz Order Fulfillment:** When you order a physical item from a Biz Seller, that seller receives only the order details, name, delivery address, and contact number needed to deliver your order.
3. **Contractually Bound Infrastructure Processors:** Trusted cloud, streaming, and payment processors—such as Google Cloud / Firebase (database, auth, storage), LiveKit (low-latency Live WebRTC relay), Flutterwave, licensed mobile-money aggregators, Apple, and Google Play—acting strictly under written **Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)** with confidentiality and security mandates.
4. **Valid Legal Process & Emergency Child/Life Protection:** We disclose non-public records to law enforcement or regulators **only** in response to a legally valid warrant, court order, or statutory mandate (such as confirmed CSAM reporting under child-protection laws), subject to human-rights and legality review under our [Law Enforcement Guidelines](LAW_ENFORCEMENT_GUIDELINES.md). Unless legally prohibited, we notify affected users prior to disclosure.
5. **Corporate Transactions:** In a merger, acquisition, or financing, data remains subject to protections at least as strong as this Privacy Policy, with advance notice to users before any material change.

| **Diagnostic, Security & Crash Logs** | Automatically rotated or anonymized within **90 to 180 days** |

## 7. Security Architecture & 72-Hour Breach Commitment

Oneova enforces defense-in-depth engineering controls:
- **Cryptographic Tenant Isolation** (`users/{uid}`) and explicit `allowedViewers` access control lists preventing cross-account data leaks;
- **Passkeys, 2FA, App PIN / Biometrics, and Active Session Controls** allowing instant remote sign-out of lost or stolen phones;
- **Zero Standing Staff Access** to private messages, audited break-glass controls, and mandatory hardware MFA across internal infrastructure;
- **Breach Notification Guarantee:** In the event of a personal data breach posing a risk to user rights and freedoms, Oneova notifies competent Data Protection Authorities within **72 hours** (or shorter national deadline) and promptly notifies affected users in plain language.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights & How to Exercise Them

We extend strong data rights to **every Oneova user worldwide**:

1. **Right of Access & Information:** Know what personal data we process about you.
2. **Right to Data Portability (Export):** Download a structured copy of your profile, settings, and content (**Me → Settings → Privacy & Security → Export Data**).
3. **Right to Rectification:** Update or correct inaccurate account details anytime.
4. **Right to Erasure (Account & Data Deletion):** Permanently delete individual items or your entire account in-app or via `https://oneova.app/deletion`.
5. **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Revoke Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, Connected Channels, or marketing notifications at any time without penalty.
6. **Right to Object / Restrict Automated Profiling:** Reset feed recommendations or opt out of personalized promotional targeting in Settings.
7. **Right to Lodge a Regulatory Complaint:** You may contact our DPO at **`dpo@oneova.app`** (free response within **21 days**, and always within statutory 30-day limits) or contact your national supervisory authority:
   - **Kenya:** Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (`odpc.go.ke`)

   - **South Africa:** Information Regulator (`inforegulator.org.za`)
   - **Tanzania:** Personal Data Protection Commission (`pdpc.go.tz`)
   - **Uganda:** Personal Data Protection Office (`pdpo.go.ug`)
   - **Rwanda:** National Cyber Security Authority — Data Protection Office (`dpo.gov.rw`)
   - **Ghana:** Data Protection Commission (`dataprotection.org.gh`)
   - **EU / EEA / UK:** Your local Data Protection Authority or UK ICO (`ico.org.uk`).
