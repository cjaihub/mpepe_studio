## 1. Definition of Confidential Information

**1.1 Scope.** **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public technical, product, security, financial, commercial, and user-related information disclosed by or on behalf of Oneova, or accessed, observed, or generated by Recipient during the Engagement, whether oral, written, visual, electronic, or in source/machine code, including without limitation:
- **(a) Source Code & Technical Architecture:** All Flutter/Dart mobile, desktop, and web code, Node/TypeScript/Python/Go/Rust backend services, Cloud Functions, Cloud Run containers, API schemas, Firestore/Storage security rules, database indexes, and network protocols;
- **(b) Creative & AI Technology:** Custom GLSL color-grading shaders, Look graphs, `.oneovapack` specifications, camera/thermal governance algorithms (`Device Advisor`), WebRTC/LiveKit broadcast pipelines, AR tracking setups, `AiSecurityGateway` architecture, AI Skills prompts, fine-tuning datasets, and recommendation weights;
- **(c) Infrastructure & Credentials:** Cloud configurations, testing environments, security audit reports, vulnerability findings, API keys, tokens, and operational runbooks;
- **(d) Business & Financial Records:** Product roadmaps, UI/UX mockups, creator monetization rates, payment-partner integrations (including M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Airtel, Orange, Flutterwave, Stripe), pricing models, marketing plans, investor decks, cap-table data, and vendor contracts; and
- **(e) User Personal Data ("Protected User Data"):** Any non-public information relating to an identified or identifiable Oneova user, including phone numbers, email addresses, private Chat messages, call metadata, KYC identity records, IP addresses, unpublished drafts, and Wallet/Coins balances.

**1.2 Exclusions.** Confidential Information (other than Protected User Data, which is **never** excluded) does not include information that Recipient proves by contemporaneous written evidence: (i) became publicly available through no breach of this Agreement; (ii) was rightfully in Recipient's possession free of confidentiality duties prior to disclosure by Oneova; or (iii) was independently developed without reference to any Oneova Confidential Information.

## 2. Strict Non-Disclosure, Non-Use & Data Security Obligations

**2.1 Duty of Strict Confidence.** Recipient shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence, use it **solely** to perform authorized duties for Oneova during the Engagement, and never disclose, publish, sell, lecture upon, or share it with any unauthorized third party.

**2.2 Source Code & AI Tool Safeguards.** Recipient shall **never**:
- Copy, fork, push, or back up Oneova code, shaders, designs, or databases to personal GitHub/GitLab accounts, personal cloud storage (personal Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox, OneDrive), personal email, messaging apps, or removable media;
- Paste Oneova source code, credentials, or Protected User Data into unapproved public AI chatbots or external tools that retain inputs for training; or
- Introduce unlicensed or viral copy-left (`AGPL`/`GPL`/`SSPL`) code into Oneova repositories without prior written Company approval.

**2.3 Absolute Protection of User Data.** Recipient acknowledges that Protected User Data is governed by strict national and international data protection statutes (including the Kenya Data Protection Act, Nigeria Data Protection Act, South Africa POPIA, Rwanda Law No. 058/2021, and EU/UK GDPR). Recipient shall access Protected User Data **only** when strictly required by an authorized job function, comply fully with the **Oneova Company Information Security Policy (`ONV-SEC-GOV-2026-V1`)**, and report any suspected security exposure to `security@oneova.app` within **15 minutes** of discovery.

**2.4 Compelled Legal Disclosure.** If Recipient is served with a court order or subpoena seeking Confidential Information, Recipient shall (where legally permitted) notify `legal@oneova.app` immediately prior to disclosure and cooperate with Oneova to seek protective treatment.

## 3. 100% Ownership & Assignment of Work Product and Inventions

**3.1 Work Made for Hire & Present Assignment.** All software code, UI/UX designs, shaders, Look packs, AI prompts, models, documentation, trademarks, audiovisual assets, inventions, discoveries, and improvements (whether patentable or copyrightable) conceived, authored, developed, or reduced to practice by Recipient (alone or with others) within the scope of the Engagement, using Oneova resources/Confidential Information, or relating to Oneova's current or demonstrably anticipated platform (**"Company Intellectual Property"**) shall be the **sole and exclusive property of Oneova Technologies Inc.** To the fullest extent permitted by law, all copyrightable works are **"works made for hire"**; to the extent any rights do not vest automatically, Recipient **hereby irrevocably assigns and transfers** to Oneova Technologies Inc. all worldwide right, title, and interest (including all copyrights, patents, trade secrets, and commercial exploitation rights) in and to all Company Intellectual Property.

**3.2 Moral Rights & Further Assurances.** To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Recipient waives any moral rights of attribution or integrity that would impede Oneova's modification, commercialization, or distribution of Company Intellectual Property, and agrees to execute any confirmatory patent/copyright filings reasonably requested by Oneova at Oneova's expense.

**3.3 Prior Inventions Carve-Out.** Any pre-existing inventions or open-source libraries owned by Recipient prior to the Engagement that Recipient wishes to exclude are listed in **Schedule A** below (if none are listed, Recipient represents there are none). Recipient shall **not** incorporate any Prior Invention into Oneova code without Oneova's prior written consent; if incorporated, Recipient grants Oneova a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, sub-licensable license to use, modify, and distribute it as part of Oneova's Services.

**3.4 No Conflicting Obligations.** Recipient warrants that signing and performing this Agreement does not breach any agreement with a former employer, university, or client, and Recipient will never bring third-party trade secrets into Oneova.

## 4. Return & Certified Deletion Upon Termination

Immediately upon termination of the Engagement (or at any time upon Oneova's written request), Recipient shall:
1. Return all Company-owned hardware, test phones, security keys, and physical records;
2. Permanently purge any local clones, caches, build artifacts, or files containing Confidential Information from any approved personal equipment; and
3. Confirm compliance in writing upon request.

## 5. Non-Solicitation & Brand Protection During Restricted Period

During the Engagement and for **twelve (12) months** thereafter (to the extent enforceable under applicable law), Recipient shall not use Oneova Confidential Information to: (a) solicit Oneova employees or core contractors to leave Oneova, or (b) misappropriate Oneova user lists, creator contact records, or private merchant directories. Nothing in this Agreement restricts lawful whistleblowing to a competent regulatory authority.

## 6. Term, Remedies & General Provisions

**6.1 Survival.** Confidentiality obligations survive termination of the Engagement for **five (5) years** as to general business information, and **perpetually** as to **Trade Secrets, Source Code, Cryptographic Architecture, and Protected User Data**. IP assignment provisions survive **perpetually**.

**6.2 Injunctive Relief.** Any breach of Sections 2 or 3 would cause irreparable harm to Oneova and its users for which monetary damages alone are inadequate. Oneova is entitled to seek immediate temporary, preliminary, and permanent injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction without the necessity of posting bond, in addition to full legal damages and recovery of reasonable legal fees.

**6.3 Governing Law & Severability.** This Agreement is governed by the laws applicable to Recipient's primary engagement contract with Oneova Technologies Inc. (or, if none is specified, the laws of the **State of Delaware, USA**, alongside mandatory local statutory employment/IP rules where Recipient resides). If any clause is held overbroad, a court shall enforce it to the maximum lawful extent while keeping all other clauses intact.
