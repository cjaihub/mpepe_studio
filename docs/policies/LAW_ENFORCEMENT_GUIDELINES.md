---

> **In Short:** Oneova cooperates with lawful investigations into serious crimes (especially child exploitation, terrorism, human trafficking, and financial fraud) while strictly defending users' constitutional privacy, due process, and freedom-of-expression rights across Africa and globally. **We never grant unfettered backdoor access, and we reject overbroad or unlawful requests.**

---

## 1. Guiding Principles

1. **Rule of Law & Due Process:** Non-public user information is disclosed **only** in response to valid, binding legal process issued by a court or competent authority with lawful jurisdiction.
2. **Specificity & Proportionality:** Every request must target specific accounts (`@username`, user UID, registered phone/email, or exact post URL) and narrow date ranges. **Blanket, bulk, or "fishing expedition" demands are rejected.**
3. **International Human Rights Review:** Requests implicating peaceful civic speech, journalism, whistleblowing, or political opposition are escalated to Senior Legal Counsel and evaluated against domestic constitutional guarantees and international human-rights norms (including the **African Charter on Human and Peoples' Rights** and the **ICCPR**).
4. **No Backdoors:** Oneova does not install government surveillance backdoors or weaken platform encryption.

---

## 2. Legal Process Required by Data Category

| Requested Record Category | Examples | Minimum Legal Process Required |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic Subscriber Records** | Username, account creation timestamp, sign-up email/phone, recent login IP | Valid statutory subpoena, production order, or court order under applicable national law |
| **Transaction & Payout Logs** | Coin purchase timestamps, payout mobile-money/bank reference IDs | Court order or statutory financial-crime / AML warrant |
| **Non-Public Account Metadata** | Device identifiers, session logs, non-public friend/follower graphs | Court order establishing specific legal relevance to an active criminal investigation |
| **Private Content** | Private Chat messages, unpublished Studio drafts, private Stories, deleted items still in retention window | **Judicial Search Warrant** (or equivalent highest-tier judicial court order) supported by probable cause / reasonable suspicion |

*Cross-Border Requests:* Authorities requesting private content held outside their domestic jurisdiction should submit requests via **Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty (MLAT)** channels, Letters Rogatory, or applicable cooperative frameworks, alongside a preservation request while diplomatic process is pending.

---

## 3. Mandatory Contents of a Valid Request

Every official submission to **`lawenforcement@oneova.app`** must:
1. Be sent from an **official government, judicial, or police email domain** (requests from personal webmail accounts are not processed);
2. Identify the requesting agency, investigating officer's name, badge/ID number, official phone number, and physical office address;
3. Attach a signed, dated, and sealed copy of the court order, subpoena, or warrant citing the specific statutory authority;
4. Specify the **exact Oneova account identifier** (`@username`, profile URL, post URL, or registered phone/email)—display names alone are insufficient because multiple users may share similar names; and
5. Specify the **exact data categories and narrow UTC date range** sought.

---

## 4. 90-Day Data Preservation Requests

We honor formal preservation requests from verified law-enforcement agencies for **90 days** (renewable once for an additional 90 days) while authorities obtain formal judicial legal process. Preservation freezes existing records in a snapshot as of receipt; it does not disclose data until valid legal process is served.

---

## 5. Emergency Disclosure Requests (Imminent Danger to Life or Child Safety)

If an authority has a good-faith belief that an **imminent emergency involving danger of death, kidnapping, child sexual exploitation, or severe physical injury** requires immediate disclosure without delay:
- Submit an official request marked **`URGENT EMERGENCY DISCLOSURE`** from an official government email domain to **`lawenforcement@oneova.app`** (and `childsafety@oneova.app` for threats to minors);
- State: (a) nature of the imminent threat to life/physical safety, (b) exact `@username` / URL involved, (c) specific minimal data needed to avert the emergency, and (d) why normal judicial process cannot be obtained in time.
- Legal & Trust leadership evaluate valid emergency requests **24/7**.

---

## 6. User Notification & Semi-Annual Transparency Report

- **Advance Notice to Users:** Oneova's policy is to **notify users before disclosing their account information**, giving them an opportunity to seek legal counsel, **except** where: (a) prohibited by a valid judicial non-disclosure/gag order, or (b) Oneova reasonably determines notice would create an immediate risk of harm to a child, threat to human life, flight from prosecution, or intimidation of witnesses.
- **Transparency Reporting:** Every six months, Oneova publishes the total number of government data and takedown requests received per country, the percentage complied with vs. rejected, and the number of accounts affected.
