## 1. Zero-Tolerance Standard Against Child Sexual Exploitation & Abuse (CSAE)

Oneova enforces an uncompromising, global **zero-tolerance policy** against Child Sexual Abuse Material (**CSAM**) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (**CSAE**), in full alignment with the **African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child (ACRWC)**, national child-protection statutes (including Kenya's Children Act & Computer Misuse Act, Nigeria's Child's Rights Act & Cybercrimes Act, South Africa's Children's Act & Cybercrimes Act, Ghana's Cybersecurity Act ss. 62–66), the **UK Online Safety Act & Children's Code**, **18 U.S.C. § 2258A**, **Apple App Store § 1.2**, and **Google Play Child Safety Standards**.

### 1.1 Strictly Prohibited Conduct (Immediate Permanent Ban)
1. **CSAM in Any Format:** Real, altered, illustrated, or **AI-generated** nude, sexually explicit, or sexually suggestive imagery, video, or audio involving any person under **18 years of age**.
2. **Online Grooming & Enticement:** Adults cultivating romantic/sexual trust with minors, requesting private images, offering mobile money, airtime, Oneova Coins, gifts, or favours for sexual interaction, or attempting to move minors to off-platform encrypted chats or physical meetings.
3. **Youth Sextortion:** Coercing or blackmailing a young person with real or fabricated intimate images. **Teen victims of sextortion are protected, supported, and never penalized.**
4. **Child Trafficking & Exploitative Recruitment:** Fake scholarships, sports academies, domestic-work placements, or modelling/influencer offers used to traffic or exploit minors.
5. **Harmful Practices Against Children:** Promoting child marriage, female genital mutilation (FGM), or physical cruelty against minors.

### 1.2 Detection, Preservation & Mandatory Reporting
- **Technical Safeguards:** Cryptographic hash-matching against known CSAM repositories, strict AI Lab prompt-level blocks, and anti-grooming behavioral signals.
- **Enforcement & Reporting:** Upon confirmation, content is quarantined immediately, offending accounts and associated device identifiers are permanently terminated, forensic records are preserved in an isolated encrypted vault, and statutory reports are filed with the **National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC CyberTipline)**, **INTERPOL**, and competent national cybercrime/child-protection agencies.

## 3. Regional Youth-Scam & Wellbeing Protections

- **Scholarship, Visa & Job-Scam Shields:** Links or DMs soliciting upfront mobile-money fees for "guaranteed scholarships/visas" trigger fraud warnings and priority moderator review.
- **Academic Integrity:** Selling leaked national examination papers or impersonating examination councils is prohibited.
- **Restricted Youth Commercial Categories:** Gambling, sports betting, alcohol, tobacco/nicotine vapes, prescription pharmaceuticals, cosmetic surgery, weight-loss supplements, and hazardous skin-lightening products may never target minors.
