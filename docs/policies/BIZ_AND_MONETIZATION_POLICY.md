## 1. Eligibility, KYC & AML/CTF Compliance

**1.1 Age & Account Standing.** To open a Biz storefront, sell `.oneovapack` digital assets, receive Live Gifts, or withdraw Creator Earnings, you must: (a) be at least **18 years old**, (b) hold an account in good standing under our [Community Guidelines](COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md), and (c) reside in a supported payout country.

**1.2 Know Your Customer (KYC) & Business Verification (KYB).** Before disbursing payouts above regulatory thresholds, our licensed payment partners verify:
- Government-issued photo identification (National ID, Passport, Voter/Alien Card, or Driver's License);
- Matching mobile-money wallet registration name (e.g., registered M-Pesa / MTN MoMo / Airtel Money subscriber name) or verified bank account in the creator/merchant's legal name; and
- Business registration / tax identification (e.g., KRA PIN, CAC/TIN Nigeria, CIPC/SARS South Africa, BRELA/TIN Tanzania, RDB Rwanda) where commercial law requires.

**1.3 Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & Sanctions Screening.** All payouts and high-velocity Coin flows are screened against global and national AML/CTF and sanctions rules (including Kenya POCAMLA, Nigeria Money Laundering Prevention & Prohibition Act, South Africa FICA, BCEAO directives, FATF standards, OFAC, UN, UK, and EU sanctions lists).

## 2. Oneova Coins, Virtual Gifts & Creator Revenue Share

**2.1 Nature of Oneova Coins.** Oneova Coins are closed-loop digital tokens redeemable solely inside Oneova for virtual Gifts and eligible digital features. Coins are **not** deposits, e-money, stablecoins, cryptocurrency, or transferable financial instruments.

**2.2 Transparent Checkout.** Coin bundle prices are displayed in the buyer's local currency inclusive of applicable VAT, Digital Services Tax (DST), and platform billing fees (Apple In-App Purchase / Google Play Billing on mobile apps where required, or licensed web/mobile-money checkout where permitted).

**2.3 Creator Payout Ledger.**
- Valid Gifts received during Live broadcasts or on eligible Reels credit the creator's **Creator Balance** at the published net revenue-share rate shown in **Creator Studio** prior to activation.
- Payout schedules, minimum withdrawal amounts, FX conversion rates, and mobile-money/bank network fees are displayed clearly before you confirm any withdrawal.
- **Anti-Wash-Trading Rule:** Using stolen cards, SIM-swapped mobile-money accounts, self-tipping rings, or chargeback cycling to launder funds results in immediate freeze of tainted credits, permanent account termination, and reporting to payment partners and financial-crime authorities.

## 3. Oneova Biz Marketplace Rules for Sellers

**3.1 Independent Seller Status.** Unless explicitly marked "Sold by Oneova", each Biz Seller is the independent seller of record responsible for product quality, lawful sourcing, tax remittance, and fulfillment.

**3.2 Mandatory Listing Standards.** Every Biz product listing must:
1. Show real, unmanipulated photos/videos of the actual item or `.oneovapack` showcase;
2. Display the **true total price** in local currency, clear delivery fees, and realistic dispatch/delivery timelines;
3. Never demand off-book "customs release fees" or surprise post-payment surcharges from buyers; and
4. Honor statutory return, replacement, and refund rights under applicable consumer law (including defective, damaged, undelivered, or materially misdescribed items).

**3.3 Studio Look Packs (`.oneovapack`).** Verified Business creators selling `.oneovapack` color-grading and LUT bundles must own 100% of the packaged LUTs/grades and include an authentic before/after showcase video. Buyers receive a non-exclusive license to apply purchased Looks to their own photos/videos inside Oneova, without redistribution rights.

## 4. Prohibited & Restricted Commerce Schedule

The following may **never** be listed, promoted, or sold on Oneova Biz or Live Shopping:
1. Counterfeit, replica, pirated, or stolen goods;
2. Firearms, ammunition, explosives, tasers, or tactical weapons;
3. Illegal narcotics, controlled substances, prescription-only medicines without licensed pharmacy authorization, or unapproved "miracle cure" supplements;
4. Hazardous cosmetics containing banned mercury, high-dose hydroquinone, or steroids;
5. Human organs, blood, body parts, or surrogacy/trafficking brokerage;
6. Protected wildlife, ivory, rhino horn, pangolin, exotic live animals, or illegal timber/charcoal;
7. Adult sexual services, escorting, pornography, or non-consensual imagery;
8. Unlicensed gambling, lottery tickets, pyramid/Ponzi investment packages, fake currencies, or forged government documents/academic certificates;
9. Personal data dumps, hacked social accounts, spyware, or SIM-cloning hardware.

## 5. Refunds, Escrow Holds & Buyer Protection

- **Digital Coins:** Final upon delivery except for unauthorized fraudulent debits, duplicate mobile-money charges (reconciled automatically via webhook ledger checks), or mandatory statutory/app-store refund rights.
- **Biz Orders:** Where in-app checkout/escrow is active, seller settlement may be held until delivery confirmation or expiry of the buyer inspection window so buyers are protected against non-delivery.
- **Dispute Escalation:** Buyers and Sellers who cannot resolve an order issue within 48 hours may escalate in-app or via `commerce@oneova.app`.
