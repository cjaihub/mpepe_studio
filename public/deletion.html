Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: October 2026 • GDPR, CCPA & TikTok Compliant

Oneova provides complete autonomy over your creative assets and account data. If you wish to delete your account, remove uploaded video reels, or unlink third-party accounts (Meta/TikTok/Google), follow the methods below.

Method 1: Instant In-App Automated Deletion (Recommended)

The fastest way to purge your entire account and media history immediately:

  1. Open the Oneova app on your iOS or Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile (Me) tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap the Settings & Privacy icon (top-right corner).
  4. Select Account Security & Privacy Controls.
  5. Tap Delete Account & Purge Media and confirm with your security PIN or biometric auth.

Your profile, followers, video reels, and audio tracks are permanently removed across all edge servers within 24 hours.

Method 2: Revoking Third-Party Publishing Permissions

If you linked TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram, or Facebook for 1-Tap Universal Sharing:

  1. Navigate to your connected social platform's Settings & Privacy → Apps & Websites.
  2. Locate Oneova in the active application permissions list.
  3. Click Revoke Access or Remove to disconnect all token permissions instantly.

Method 3: Email Verification & Manual Deletion Request

To submit a manual data erasure request via our privacy officer:

  1. Send an email to: privacy@oneova.app
  2. Subject Line: Data Deletion Request - [Your Username or Phone]
  3. Our trust team will verify your ownership and complete the total database purge within 48 hours.
