Executive Summary ("In Short"):
1. We never sell your personal data—not your phone number, not your contacts, not your private chats, and not your videos.
2. We never store mobile-money PINs (M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Airtel, Orange, Wave) or raw card CVVs.

5. 2-Tap Control: Access, export, or permanently delete your account anytime in Me → Settings → Privacy & Security.

1. Scope and Global + African Data Protection Standards

This Privacy Policy explains how Oneova Technologies Inc. collects, uses, protects, shares, and deletes personal data across the Oneova mobile, desktop, and web applications (Reels, Stories, Chat & Calls, 4K Live, Studio, AI Lab, Ova Assistant, Wallet & Coins, and Biz Marketplace).

We engineer our privacy practices to comply with leading African and global data protection laws, including:

  • Kenya: Data Protection Act, 2019 (ODPC)
  • Nigeria: Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), 2023 (NDPC)
  • South Africa: Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA — Information Regulator)
  • Tanzania: Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 (PDPC)
  • Uganda: Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 (PDPO)
  • Rwanda: Law No. 058/2021 Relating to the Protection of Personal Data and Privacy (NCSA)
  • Ghana: Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843 — DPC)
  • Francophone & North Africa: Côte d'Ivoire Law No. 2013-450 (ARTCI), Senegal Data Protection Law (CDP), Egypt PDPL No. 151/2020, Morocco Law 09-08 (CNDP), Ethiopia Proclamation No. 1321/2024, and the African Union Malabo Convention
  • Global: EU/EEA & UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California (CCPA/CPRA), and Brazil (LGPD).

Strict Exclusions — Data We NEVER Collect

  1. Never Mobile-Money PINs or Card CVVs: We never request, see, or store your M-Pesa PIN, MTN MoMo PIN, Airtel/Orange PIN, bank password, or raw payment card CVV. All payment authentication happens inside regulated PCI-DSS / central-bank-licensed gateways.
  2. Never Covert Recording: Oneova never activates your camera or microphone in the background without an active recording, call, Live broadcast, or explicit voice-assistant session initiated by you.
  3. Never Unconsented Contact Harvesting: Your phone address book is never uploaded unless you explicitly grant Contacts permission.

3. How We Protect AI, Biometrics & Facial Landmarks

3.1 On-Device AR & Beauty Processing. When you use Studio Looks, Boost Beauty, or AR camera lenses, facial/pose landmark alignment runs in volatile device memory in real time to position visual effects. Oneova does not build or sell a facial-recognition identity database from camera effects.

3.2 App Biometric Lock. When you enable Fingerprint or Face Unlock in Privacy & Security, authentication is performed strictly by your operating system's secure hardware enclave (Android Keystore / Apple Secure Enclave). Oneova never receives or stores your fingerprint image or facial scan.

3.3 AI Firewall & Zero Private Training. Inputs handled by Oneova AI tools pass through AiSecurityGateway prompt-injection isolation. Private Chat messages, calls, KYC documents, and unpublished Studio drafts are never used to train generative AI models or shared with external advertisers.

4. How and When Information Is Shared

We never sell or rent personal data to data brokers or third parties. We share limited data only in five strictly controlled situations:

  1. According to Your Audience Settings: Other users see your profile, public Reels, Live broadcasts, or Biz store according to whether you choose Public, Followers Only, or Private.
  2. Biz Order Fulfillment: When you order a physical item from a Biz Seller, that seller receives only the order details, name, delivery address, and contact number needed to deliver your order.
  3. Contractually Bound Infrastructure Processors: Trusted cloud, streaming, and payment processors—such as Google Cloud / Firebase (database, auth, storage), LiveKit (low-latency Live WebRTC relay), Flutterwave, Stripe, licensed mobile-money aggregators, Apple, and Google Play—acting strictly under written Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with confidentiality and security mandates.
  4. Valid Legal Process & Emergency Child/Life Protection: We disclose non-public records to law enforcement or regulators only in response to a legally valid warrant, court order, or statutory mandate (such as confirmed CSAM reporting under child-protection laws), subject to human-rights and legality review under our Law Enforcement Guidelines. Unless legally prohibited, we notify affected users prior to disclosure.
  5. Corporate Transactions: In a merger, acquisition, or financing, data remains subject to protections at least as strong as this Privacy Policy, with advance notice to users before any material change.

5. International & Intra-African Data Transfers

Because Oneova connects creators and businesses across Africa and the world, personal data may be processed on secure cloud servers outside your country of residence. Whenever data is transferred internationally, we implement legally recognized transfer safeguards:

  • Adequacy & Regulator Whitelists recognized under your national data protection law;
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and binding Data Transfer Agreements enforcing equivalent technical and legal protections;
  • Statutory Registration & Prior Authorization where required for specific data categories under Kenya DPA Part VI, Nigeria NDPA Part VI, South Africa POPIA s.72, Tanzania PDPA, Rwanda Law No. 058/2021, Côte d'Ivoire ARTCI, and Morocco CNDP; and
  • End-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.

7. Security Architecture & 72-Hour Breach Commitment

Oneova enforces defense-in-depth engineering controls:

  • Cryptographic Tenant Isolation (users/{uid}) and explicit allowedViewers access control lists preventing cross-account data leaks;
  • Passkeys, 2FA, App PIN / Biometrics, and Active Session Controls allowing instant remote sign-out of lost or stolen phones;
  • Zero Standing Staff Access to private messages, audited break-glass controls, and mandatory hardware MFA across internal infrastructure;
  • Breach Notification Guarantee: In the event of a personal data breach posing a risk to user rights and freedoms, Oneova notifies competent Data Protection Authorities within 72 hours (or shorter national deadline) and promptly notifies affected users in plain language.

8. Your Privacy Rights & How to Exercise Them

We extend strong data rights to every Oneova user worldwide:

  1. Right of Access & Information: Know what personal data we process about you.
  2. Right to Data Portability (Export): Download a structured copy of your profile, settings, and content (Me → Settings → Privacy & Security → Export Data).
  3. Right to Rectification: Update or correct inaccurate account details anytime.
  4. Right to Erasure (Account & Data Deletion): Permanently delete individual items or your entire account in-app or via https://oneova.app/deletion.
  5. Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, Connected Channels, or marketing notifications at any time without penalty.
  6. Right to Object / Restrict Automated Profiling: Reset feed recommendations or opt out of personalized promotional targeting in Settings.
  7. Right to Lodge a Regulatory Complaint: You may contact our DPO at dpo@oneova.app (free response within 21 days, and always within statutory 30-day limits) or contact your national supervisory authority:
  • Kenya: Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke)
  • Nigeria: Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng)
  • South Africa: Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za)
  • Tanzania: Personal Data Protection Commission (pdpc.go.tz)
  • Uganda: Personal Data Protection Office (pdpo.go.ug)
  • Rwanda: National Cyber Security Authority — Data Protection Office (dpo.gov.rw)
  • Ghana: Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.org.gh)
  • EU / EEA / UK: Your local Data Protection Authority or UK ICO (ico.org.uk).

9. Children's & Teen Privacy

Oneova does not permit accounts for children under 13. Users aged 13 to 17 receive privacy-by-design defaults—including private-by-default profiles for ages 13–15, mutual-follower DM protections, disabled targeted ad profiling, and blocked Wallet/Live monetization. Full details are set out in our Child & Teen Safety Policy.
