Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Global GDPR & CCPA Verified

At Oneova, we hold creator privacy and intellectual property to the highest industry standards. This policy outlines how your data, media streams, and device telemetry are safeguarded when using the Oneova mobile app and creator suite.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential data necessary to deliver low-latency live streaming and creative mobile workflows:

  • Account & Profile Credentials: Username, display handle, avatar, and authentication details required for verified creator badges.
  • Real-Time Live Streaming Data: Video and audio camera feeds captured during 4K Live Broadcasts and Creator Rooms. Streams are routed in real-time with zero unauthorized archival.
  • Mobile Editor Telemetry: Timeline edit data and cut parameters. AI Subject Cutouts run locally on device hardware whenever available to keep your source clips private.

2. Data Usage & Protection

Collected information is used strictly to power high-performance creator capabilities:

  • Delivering zero-buffering 60 FPS Reels feeds and personalized creator recommendations.
  • Facilitating interactive split-screen PK Battles, virtual gifting, and real-time chat interactions.
  • Enabling 1-Tap Universal Sharing to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and Facebook with preserved attribution.
  • We do not sell personal data, facial biometrics, or video assets to third-party data brokers.

3. Third-Party Platform Publishing

When you use Oneova's Universal 1-Tap Sharer to publish reels across social platforms:

  • We transmit only the specific exported video, captions, and tags that you explicitly authorize.
  • Account credentials are exchanged via secure, encrypted OAuth tokens.
  • You can revoke connected platform permissions at any time inside app settings.

4. Data Deletion & Creator Autonomy

You maintain complete ownership of your creative footprint:

  • You may request immediate account deletion directly from the mobile app (Profile → Settings → Delete Account) or via our Data Deletion Instructions.
  • Upon deletion confirmation, all user data, videos, drafts, and followers are purged from production clusters within 24 hours.

5. Contact & Trust Team

For questions or privacy inquiries, contact our Trust & Safety Officer at: privacy@oneova.app
